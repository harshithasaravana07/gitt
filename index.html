Taking care of nature starts with simple everyday choices.

🌱 Plant more
Grow native plants and trees in your community.
💧 Save water

♻️ Reduce waste
Reuse items, recycle correctly, and avoid single-use plastics.

Keep natural habitats clean and let wild animals live undisturbed.
